## 1. Qubits & Superposition

Unlike classical bits constrained to 0 or 1 state vectors, a quantum bit (qubit) exists in a coherent superposition state:

$$|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$$

where $|\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1$.

## 2. Superconducting Transmon Hardware

Superconducting quantum processors utilize Josephson junctions to form non-harmonic LC oscillators, allowing microwave pulses at ~5 GHz to selectively address energy transitions between $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$ energy levels.
